How to read this board

Implied value is the model's claim for what a protocol's token would be worth if its record stayed as it is — TVL × 0.30, adjusted for 7-day growth, age and chain reach. It is not a prediction that a token will launch, or when. “Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — never that the protocol has none. Wrapped, staked and bridged products are not ranked at all: their token already trades in another form, so they sit in the “Not ranked” list below instead of at the top of the board. Not ranked — wrapped, staked and bridged products

These protocols record no token of their own, but each is a wrapped, staked or bridged form of a token that already trades — WBTC is wrapped BTC, Binance staked ETH is staked ETH. A launch of their own would not be a new token, so the board does not rank them. They are listed here, by TVL, so nothing is hidden.
